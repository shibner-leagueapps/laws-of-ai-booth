Team Builder needed fast, flexible filtering across multiple data sources — but performance dropped as combinations increased. Understanding real-world data was critical before building the MVP.

I asked AI to explore our databases, map relationships between user profiles, registrations, and custom form fields, and help categorize what properties actually apply to players.

AI connected data across services, surfaced patterns quickly, and answered follow-up questions — work that used to take hours or days of manual querying. This:

  1. Saved hours of analysis and days of potential development time

  2. Validated that MVP filtering could stay simple

  3. Let us defer complex optimizations until real usage revealed edge cases
