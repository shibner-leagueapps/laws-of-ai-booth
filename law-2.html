Second Law of AI

AI works better if you give it clear instructions. Including simple directions in your prompts for things like format, tone, or length help turn vague results into something more usable.


Weak example:

“Write an email response.”


Strong approach:

“Write a friendly, 3-sentence email asking to reschedule a meeting.”
